Royals Offseason Training

Your plan for Nov 30 – Feb 28

A personal training plan built from Coach Brandon's training philosophy. It sets each day's run and pace based on your experience and a recent race, and lets you log your runs for the coaches to see.

The plan runs through Feb 28, but track practice will likely start before then, in mid-to-late February. Once it does, follow your track workouts. You can still use this then to log your runs.

How to use it

Five steps. Takes about two minutes to set up.

  1. Create your account

    Open the app and tap Create an account. Use your first name and last initial (like Jordan R) and pick a 4-digit PIN.

    You'll use your name and PIN to sign in on any phone or computer.

  2. Fill in your info

    Enter your grade, years running, a recent race time, your biggest week this season (in minutes or miles), and how much you've run since state.

    The app builds your plan from that. Tap any week at the top to see it.

  3. Follow your plan

    Each day shows a time range, an easy-pace guide, and how hard it should feel. Easy days should feel easy.

    Busy day? Tap Move to trade days. The app never puts two hard days back to back.

  4. Log every run

    After you run, tap Log it and enter your minutes, effort, and how it went. Your logs go to a team database your coaches can see.

    Use Strava? You can paste a link to your run too (optional). In Strava, open the run, tap Share, then Copy link, and paste it in the Strava link box.

    If something hurts, check the box and tell a coach in person.

  5. Can't run? That's OK.

    Tap Can't run and pick a reason so Coach knows what happened.

    Missing one run won't hurt your fitness. Don't add the minutes to other days. Just pick up the plan the next day.

Tips

  • Add it to your home screen. iPhone: open the app in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: menu → Add to Home screen.
  • Copy week copies the week's plan as text, so you can paste it into Notes, your calendar, or wherever you want.
  • Stick to the plan. Running more than it says won't make you faster, and it raises your chance of getting hurt.
  • Forgot your PIN? Ask a coach to reset it.
