MLK Cross Country

MSCCA Awards Banquet

Key details for honored athletes and their families

Who is invited?

  • Brent Ferrell
  • Miles Fowler
  • Dillon Fell
  • Vicki Grammer

Key details

Location
Brentwood Academy
219 Granny White Pike, Brentwood, TN
Signs by the sidewalk will point you to the dining hall.
Date
Monday, November 9, 2026
Time
Dinner starts at 6:00 PM, with awards after.
Cost
  • Free for MSCCA coaches and the athletes being honored.
  • $15 per person for all other guests (cash, Venmo, or check).
  • Schools covering their extra guests can make checks out to MSCCA.
Guest limit
Space is tight, so please bring no more than 2 guests per runner if possible. A family that needs to bring more is OK, but there is no room for extended family or teammates who aren’t being honored.
Dress
“Sunday best” for athletes. It isn’t formal, but they should look nice and presentable.
