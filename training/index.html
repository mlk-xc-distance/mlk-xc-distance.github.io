Why this week looks like this

    Your paces

    Coach Brandon's ground rules

    • Easy days EASY. Recovery runs at effort 2–3, never over 3.
    • Run by feel, not the watch. Paces are a guide; effort matters more.
    • Time, not miles. Your body knows minutes. Aerobic runs need at least 25 minutes to count.
    • Build slowly. Raise weekly volume 10–15% at most, hold it 2–3 weeks, then take a down week.
    • Rest is training. Overracing burns out young runners far more often than overtraining does.
    • Race the races, not the workouts. No time trials in practice, and no extra reps.
